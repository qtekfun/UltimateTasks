UltimateTasks is a friendly app for the tasks you keep in <b>Nextcloud</b> (CalDAV), inspired by Apple Reminders. It keeps working without a connection and syncs when it can.

<b>At a glance</b>
<ul>
<li>Today, Scheduled, All and Completed, then your lists, and search across everything.</li>
<li>Round checkboxes with undo, notes, due date and time, priority, tags, link and subtasks.</li>
<li>Choose which lists to show: the task lists Nextcloud Deck publishes stay out of the way unless you want them.</li>
<li>Create, rename, recolor and reorder lists; delete them only if you turn that on in Settings.</li>
</ul>

<b>Repeating tasks and reminders that arrive</b>
<ul>
<li>Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly or custom, including "the 3rd Friday of every month".</li>
<li>Exact reminders, early reminders and snooze from the notification.</li>
<li>A wizard for phones that stop apps in the background (Xiaomi, Oppo, OnePlus, Realme, Huawei, Honor, Samsung, vivo) and an alarm-clock mode no battery saver delays.</li>
</ul>

<b>Offline first</b>
<ul>
<li>Every change is saved at once and synced later, even if you close the app.</li>
<li>If a title or notes changed both here and on the server, you choose which to keep: nothing is overwritten silently.</li>
</ul>

<b>Yours</b>
<ul>
<li>Light, dark and pure black themes, Material You colors, English and Spanish; two or three panes on tablets.</li>
<li>Export your settings, and optionally your session protected by a password, to move to a new phone.</li>
<li>HTTPS only, sign in through Nextcloud's Login Flow, app password encrypted with the Android Keystore.</li>
<li>Free software (GPL-3.0-or-later). No Google services, no ads, no tracking: your data only goes to your own Nextcloud.</li>
</ul>

Requires a Nextcloud server with tasks in CalDAV (the Tasks app, Calendar or Deck).
